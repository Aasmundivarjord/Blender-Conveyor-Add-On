Lay out warehouse conveyor lines in minutes. Draw a line piece by piece and
the add-on builds a render-ready conveyor with Geometry Nodes: frame, rollers
or belt, guide rails, legs and goods that move along it.

**Build**
- New Line at Cursor, then just press the arrow keys: up adds a straight,
  left and right a curve, down opens the piece library; Backspace takes the
  last piece off. Shift + arrows adjust the piece just added (length,
  radius, turn), Ctrl + up / down its height (Cmd on a Mac). Straights, curves (30–180°), inclines with automatic
  nose-overs, spirals.
- The viewport is the drawing: every piece shows its length, angle and
  radius CAD style. Click a dimension to type a value, or drag it.
- Catalog-like pieces: straights in 150 mm steps, standard widths, typical
  curve radii. Conveyor types are Roller (MDR), Belt, Modular plastic belt
  and an overhead Pouch sorter.

**Connect**
- Dock a line onto another and a merge or diverter is built in its place,
  with the technology you choose: angled roller merge, pop-up belt, wheel
  or chain transfer, high-performance divert, angled-roller belt or pusher.
- Right-angle transfers work as four-way nodes: dock lines on any free side
  and they share the transfer's stands.
- Vertical conveyors (S/C), spirals, angled-roller align and turn units,
  pallet wrappers and accumulation buffers are modules you drop into a line.

**Animate**
- Tick Populate and press Play: placeholder totes, or any object you pick,
  ride the line. They stop and lift on transfers, split at diverters by
  share or class, merge without overlapping, and queue in buffers that fill
  up and release first in, first out.
- Two sliders run the flow: Population (how full the stream is) and Speed.
  The time scale lets a shift play out in seconds.

**Details**
- Legs at a spacing you choose land on whatever floor is below. Mark
  mezzanine floors, or hang a line from the ceiling. Take single stands
  out or add extra ones with − and + buttons in the viewport.
- Five material slots per line, readable node trees with numbered stages,
  and light and dark theme-aware overlays.

Requires Blender 5.2 LTS or newer. No external software, no network
access. Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).
